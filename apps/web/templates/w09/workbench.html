How raw values, scaling and distances work

Raw numerator rates and governed minutes

Raw feature values are retained event numerator counts per 90 governed minutes; they are not percentages. Accurate Passes Per90, Shots On Target Per90 and Duels Won Per90 remain numerator rates, not pass-completion, shot-accuracy or duel-win percentages.

For this retained historical authority, governed-minute denominators are conservative lower bounds, so any per-90 rate may be overstated.

Goals Per90 excludes recorded event-9 save-attempt actions. The retained fields do not support an outcome or goalkeeper-quality interpretation of those excluded actions.

Frozen global scaling

The pinned index fits one scaler over every eligible matrix row: scaled = (raw − global median) / global IQR. A zero global IQR uses a unit scale of 1. Position, target-competition/season and minimum-minute filters do not refit the scaler.

One selected target population

A full-population query scores every row admitted by the explicit filters in one selected target competition and season before applying the result limit. The exemplar may come from another competition. This surface does not combine all leagues into one candidate pool.

Weighted Euclidean

Each feature contribution is weight × scaled contrast². Contributions are non-negative, and distance is the square root of their sum.

Weighted cosine

Each signed contribution is the negative product of the weighted normalised query and candidate components. Distance is 1 plus the sum of those signed contributions.

Interpretation boundary

Lower distance means closer only under the same method, features, weights and authority pins. Distances, per-feature contributions and weights are not percentages, probabilities or calibrated match scores.
