What this information cannot tell you

Counts describe recorded actions, not whether those actions were effective. Percentages describe the share of recorded actions in a category. Differences may reflect role, tactics, opposition, opportunity or data coverage; they do not establish cause.

This information cannot establish off-ball behaviour, tactical instructions, opponent adjustments, current or future ability, availability, squad fit, value or recruitment outcomes. Goalkeeper action categories do not establish save or shot-stopping quality.
