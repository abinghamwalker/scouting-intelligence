Before you decide

What formal participation involves

Time: approximately 30–35 minutes. You will assess 8 query sets: 80 blinded primary candidates plus 2 delayed, blinded repeat assessments, for 82 responses in total.

Who is eligible: at least 2 years in one or more accepted professional football roles, player assessment within the last 5 years, and no material conflict involving a displayed player or club. The formal study needs at least 5 eligible experts.

Each task: choose football relevance from 0–4 and confidence from 1–5, or explicitly abstain / mark unable to assess. All 82 presentations need an explicit response. Before immutable submission, you can review response values and retain append-only corrections.

Retained locally: eligibility, consent, ratings, confidence, optional football explanations, timestamps, and a one-way participant-code digest in local SQLite and immutable local evidence files. No name or contact detail is requested.

How the final decision is made

PASS requires exact authority and complete coverage, precision@5 ≥ 0.60, mean NDCG@5 ≥ 0.65, retrieved-control relevant-rate lift ≥ 0.20, paired NDCG@5 delta ≥ 0.05 with its paired 95% query-bootstrap lower bound above 0, ordinal agreement ≥ 0.40, repeat mean absolute difference ≤ 1.0, and repeat within-one rate ≥ 0.80.

FAIL means stale/substituted authority, protected-label leakage or reuse, or a complete compatible formal study missing any preregistered PASS threshold.

INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE means approval/evidence is absent, fewer than 5 eligible experts complete all 82 responses, required query/candidate coverage or 3 non-abstaining raters per candidate is missing, fewer than 80% of expected repeat pairs have two rated answers, or a required metric has no valid denominator.

Pilot versus formal: the mechanics pilot uses only the first 2 query sets to rehearse the interface. It is never formal expert evidence, never enters formal files or metrics, and cannot unlock a gate.
