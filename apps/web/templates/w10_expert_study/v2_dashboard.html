About this form

A trial of a historical player-comparison research form

This is a trial of a historical player-comparison research form. You will review five pairs of players and answer one question about each pair. We are testing whether the football information in the form is clear and sufficient for a professional judgement.

Your responses

Your answers are saved on this computer under a participant code rather than your name. They are not sent over the internet. Each comparison is saved as you continue, so an unfinished form can resume on this computer.

Stopping and submitting

You can stop and withdraw before final submission. After you press “Submit final answers,” the stored response is locked and cannot be edited through this form.

Research boundary

This research is about historical playing style. It is not recruitment advice and must not be used as a recommendation about a player.

Before you begin

This trial is for people with at least two years of relevant professional football experience who have assessed players professionally within the last five years.
