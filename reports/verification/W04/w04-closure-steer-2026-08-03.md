# W04 closure steer — 2026-08-03

## Controlling decision

This user-issued steer supersedes any broader interpretation of the standing
bounded-correction authority for the remainder of W04. The current R12 correction
must close the six failures demonstrated by the latest complete gate, including
the exact foreign-cache denial, mechanically derived aggregate preimages and
constants, Darwin empty-directory link-count correction, and focused adversarial
tests already in progress.

After the R12 candidate passes its focused tests, a new finding blocks W04 only if
it reproducibly demonstrates at least one of these conditions:

1. accepted executable code or an authority identity can be substituted;
2. Bronze, Silver, Gold, manifest, receipt, or digest bytes can be incorrect;
3. source completeness, data rights, temporal safety, or local-only controls can
   be bypassed;
4. required evidence can claim success when execution actually failed; or
5. a P0/P1 security defect has a reproducible exploitation path.

Incidental host-state variation, including unrelated PYC presence/cache tags,
inode/link-count variation, empty-directory metadata, temporary paths, timestamps,
or equivalent platform details, cannot create W04 authority, enter a digest,
expand a roster, or start another W04 rework cycle unless it meets one of those
five blocker tests. A non-blocking platform-assurance finding is recorded
explicitly as deferred W10 hardening, receives a regression test where bounded
and useful, and is not a W04 acceptance dependency. Failing tests remain visible
and cannot be waived or concealed.

## Fixed closure sequence

After focused R12 PASS, execute exactly:

1. fresh independent R12 review;
2. master R12 acceptance;
3. one complete-repository and W04 phase gate;
4. required health/evidence readback; and
5. registry reconciliation and the prescribed W04 checkpoint commits/tag.

No new runtime-control revision is permitted merely to strengthen already-
sufficient evidence. A further revision requires a newly proven five-test
blocker. This steer does not authorize a logical model, roster, feature, product,
source/right, digest-meaning, external-system, dependency, destructive, or other
user-boundary change.

## Terminal runtime-hardening clarification

A subsequent same-day user steer makes the currently running R12 candidate the
terminal W04 runtime-hardening cycle. No R13 or other W04 runtime-control authority
may be opened. After the already-running suite returns, further correction is
permitted only for a reproducible P0/P1 defect that affects executable admission,
product correctness, data rights, temporal safety, or truthful completion
evidence. All remaining host-specific PYC/cache-tag/inode/link-count/empty-
directory or equivalent filesystem-hardening concerns become explicit W10 work;
their evidence and useful regression tests remain retained and visible, but they
are removed from the W04 blocking gate.

Accepted runtime R11 and retained real-root R3 evidence are the minimum W04
operational baseline. The terminal closure sequence is therefore one final fresh
independent R12 review, one master complete-repository/W04 phase gate and health
readback, registry reconciliation, and the prescribed checkpoint commits/tag.
There is no additional W04 real-root producer/review loop.
