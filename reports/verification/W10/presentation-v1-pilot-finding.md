## Observation

The first pilot task asked for a football role/style relevance rating between T. Strakosha and
S. Handanović. The participant view supplied name, position, competition, team and evidenced
minutes. It did not supply per-90 playing features, position-relative context, action evidence,
coverage details or another substantive description of how either player played.

The product owner correctly identified that minutes measure evidence quantity and cannot support a
real player-style judgement. Selecting a relevance number would therefore measure name recognition
or guessing, not the preregistered construct.
